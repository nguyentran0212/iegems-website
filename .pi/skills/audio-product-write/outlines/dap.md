# DAP Review Outline

The DAP outline is heavier than IEM or DAC outlines — the OS layer is a full additional evaluation surface. Sections are mandatory unless the assessment genuinely has nothing to say on a topic.

Artwork placement is specified at the end of each section.

---

## 1. Preambles

```
Preambles
===

- In this review, I use the term "source" to denote a DAC+amp combo for brevity and convenience.
- Sources do not make sounds. Therefore, when I say sources "sound" a certain way, I talk about the change they make to my IEMs and earphones.
- I want my music to be crisp, clear, well-separated and form a 3D soundstage around my head. Sources that intensify those characteristics of my IEMs are considered "better".
- **This review is based on a review sample from [Provider] (Thank you!).** I have no affiliation with or financial interest in [Manufacturer].
- The unit retails for **[Price]**. Unaffiliated link: [URL]
```

Use "review sample from [Provider]" for tour/loan units, or "I purchased this unit on my own" for self-bought. For retail price, use the figure from the session file (or `[VERIFY]` if not confirmed).

---

## 2. General Information

400–700 words. Three parts:
- **Positioning:** Where the DAP sits in the manufacturer's product family. A brief walkthrough of the lineup is appropriate context.
- **Audio side:** DAC chip, SoC, amp stage, power supply architecture. Reference the research file's confirmed specs.
- **Android side:** SoC, RAM, storage, screen size and resolution, OS version, battery capacity.

If the research file captured manufacturer spec-sheet images, embed them here with brief captions.

**Artwork:** One or more `design-vision` slide from the manufacturer's marketing material (product positioning graphic or family lineup) if available.

---

## 3. Functionality

250–400 words. Each operating mode gets a subheading if Gen tested it: Android mode, Pure Audio mode, USB DAC mode, Bluetooth mode.

Report what Gen tested and what Gen heard or observed in each mode.

**Artwork:** One or more `generic` slide showing the DAP in the mode most relevant to the review (e.g. Android home screen, Pure Audio UI).

---

## 4. Software

250–500 words. Cover the Android features Gen found notable:
- Audio stack (system-wide SRC bypass, bit-perfect mode, exclusive audio mode)
- MSEB / tone controls if present
- PEQ and DSP plugins if supported
- Support settings
- Any notable Android quirks or limitations

Gen's voice is often playful or opinionated on software. Match it.

**Artwork:** One or more `generic` slide showing a relevant software UI element (MSEB screen, EQ screen, DSP plugin).

---

## 5. Battery Life

200–400 words. Three sub-sections if Gen has data:
- **Runtime:** Report in hours at a defined workload (e.g. AccuBattery, mixed usage, 1-hour workload, defined volume level). Cite real numbers, not vendor claims.
- **Standby:** drain rate if measured.
- **Charging:** charge speed and method.

---

## 6. Sonic Performance

400–700 words. Open with a **Test setup** block listing the IEMs used, cables, ear tips, and listening volume level.

Then:
- **Tonality and Technical Performance:** perceived tonality, how it changes the IEM, staging character, resolution. Reference tracks from `/test-tracks`.
- **Driveability:** how the DAP handles easy-to-drive IEMs, high-sensitivity IEMs (hissing check), and difficult-to-drive IEMs. Compare against the Apple USB-C dongle baseline.
- **IEM pairing notes:** any notable interactions with specific IEMs (tip-roll differences, cable effects, etc.).

**Artwork:** One or more `generic` slide showing the DAP paired with a representative IEM.

---

## 7. Selected Comparisons

One to three sources (other DAPs, DACs, or the Apple dongle baseline), each ~150–200 words. Use the Sonic Quality Rating scale: Apple dongle (2/5), generic audiophile USB DAC from 2023 (3/5), desktop DAC (4/5), anything better than desktop (5/5).

**Artwork:** One or more `generic` slide per comparison source if available. Caption: "[Source name]."

---
