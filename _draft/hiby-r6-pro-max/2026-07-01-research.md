## Audio architecture

The dual ES9039MS PRO is ESS's current flagship mobile DAC (32-bit, native DSD, current-output). In a dual mono configuration, each channel gets a dedicated chip, with a corresponding analog stage for each. The rest of the architecture is in the tech-claims section below; it is described in vendor marketing terms, not in audio-engineering primer terms.

## Manufacturer technology claims

Vendor claims as written, with a one-line skeptical framing per claim.

- **Dual ES9039MS Pro.** Vendor uses "Cutting-edge DAC Android DAP" and "Dual ES9039MS PRO." Translation: dual mono flagship-class DAC chips. Genuine; ESS's ES9039MS Pro is current mobile-flagship silicon.

- **8-way I/V Amplification.** Vendor wording: "8-way I/V Amplification." Translation: separate I/V (current-to-voltage) conversion paths. The phrase "8-way" is not industry-standard; it usually denotes four I/V stages per channel in a fully balanced design, but HiBy does not confirm. [VERIFY] exact I/V topology.

- **Flagship-grade analog components.** Vague; "flagship" is comparative. Translation: marketing sentence, not a claim that can be verified from the vendor page.

- **Four-way Independent Power Supply System.** Translation: separate power rails (digital, DAC analog, headphone amp analog, etc.) so noise from one domain does not leak into another. Standard approach in TOTL DAPs; HiBy markets it as a feature, not unique.

- **0.0003% THD.** Headline figure. Translation: aggressive marketing number that almost certainly refers to a specific load, output level, and frequency (typically THD+N at 1 kHz, mid-level, into a high-impedance load). Real-world THD under normal conditions is higher. [VERIFY] the conditions.

- **Hexagonal Warrior (design language).** Vendor wording: "Hexagonal Warrior." Aesthetic body-finish language. Subjective; not a technical claim.

- **8,200 mAh Large Battery.** Accurate per spec. The "large" framing is comparative; 8,200 mAh is large for a DAP.

- **40W PD3.0 Fast Charging.** Translation: USB Power Delivery 3.0 spec, up to 40W negotiated. Real-world charging time depends on the charger used and the charge state. HiBy sells a 65W GaN charger separately, so the DAP is not bundled with a charger capable of the full 40W rate. Worth noting.

- **Qualcomm Snapdragon 665.** The system-on-chip. Translation: An Android smartphone-class SoC from 2019. Capable but old; means R6 Pro Max runs a phone-grade Android stack, not a lightweight RTOS. Real performance depends on HiBy OS app optimisation. [VERIFY] any HiBy-bypassed mode that re-routes the signal away from Android's mixer.

- **6GB RAM + 128GB ROM.** System memory and built-in storage. Standard for the Android-class DAP category.

- **Open Android 12.** Translation: relatively unmodified Android 12 with HiBy's own app for bit-perfect playback. The "open" framing is anti-Apple, but Android is the default for DAPs in 2024-2025 anyway.

- **System-wide SRC bypass.** Translation: HiBy mode that disables Android's sample-rate conversion, sending bit-perfect audio to the DAC. Genuine for HiBy OS users. Audience relevance: critical for listeners who care about bit-perfect playback.

- **Femtosecond-grade audio oscillators.** Translation: low-jitter crystal oscillators (femtosecond-level phase noise). Standard claim across TOTL DAPs.

- **DSD1024 PCM768.** Maximum sample-rate support per vendor. Implementation depends on software path (HiBy OS app vs. third-party Android apps). [VERIFY] that all DSD1024 / PCM768 paths actually reach the DAC without downsample.

- **MQA 16x unfolding.** Full MQA decode. Requires HiBy OS app or compatible third-party app.

- **5.9 inch 1080p Large Display.** Resolution likely 1920 x 1080 or similar; [VERIFY] the exact pixel count.

- **Dual curves design.** Aesthetic language for body shape.

- **HiByCast System-wide Remote Control.** Translation: HiBy's phone app that controls the DAP over Wi-Fi / Bluetooth remote, including screen mirroring on some HiBy devices. Convenience feature; no audio impact.
