## Driver topology

- **R2R Network:** High-precision resistor network for digital-to-analogue conversion. High-speed electronic switches drive voltage-dividing resistors within the network, where different voltage divisions precisely convert an analog signal matching the original encoding.

## Manufacturer technology claims

- **Darwin III R2R Architecture:**
    - Claims to be a "Breakthrough" and "national invention patent" (Patent No.: ZL202110679801.X).
    - Uses a 1024-tap FIR filter.
    - Claims to reach "new heights in accuracy" using an R2R architecture.
    - *Translation:* High-precision R2R network with advanced digital filtering to improve linearity and accuracy.

- **Adaptive Amplification (Hybrid Class A/AB):**
    - Claims to combine the strengths of Class A and Class AB.
    - Uses the FPGA to "predict" transients (e.g., drum hits) and adjusts power output 20ms in advance.
    - Switches to Class A for high-power transients to maintain peak power and linear response.
    - Relaxes back to Class AB for "relaxed portraits" (lower average power) to lower heat and power consumption.
    - *Translation:* A dynamic switching amplifier that uses predictive logic to balance the high-fidelity/high-power output of Class A with the efficiency/lower heat of Class AB.

- **Power Supply:**
    - Independent power supplies for each channel to eliminate crosstalk and provide a "cleaner background".
    - Series-connected step-down transformer circuits to increase the overall power supply interference rejection ratio (PSRR).
    - High-voltage series-connected battery (46.8Wh) provides a steady basis for strong power output.
    - *Translation:* Significant engineering on power isolation and stability, specifically for R2R networks which are sensitive to power supply noise.

- **High FPGA Capacity:**
    - 100K logic units and 212 high-speed DSP units.
    - Used for real-time "Resistor parameter" modeling, Adaptive AMP control, and the Sankofa AI simulation.

- **Sankofa / AI Tonality Simulation:**
    - Uses a trained AI model to clone and simulate sonic characteristics of classic audio decks (e.g., MS100, MD-7831, DR7, etc.).
    - Powered by the Qualcomm Dragonwing OC88550 processor.
    - *Translation:* Software-based "virtual" hardware simulation of vintage playback devices.
