## Driver topology

Per vendor (and consistent across Hifigo + AFUL official):

- **1 dynamic driver.** Role: not specified explicitly by AFUL, but standard in hybrid IEMs of this layout is subbass / midbass duty. `[VERIFY]` which specific frequency band the DD handles, since the typical 1DD + 6BA + 1 PR + 1 MPD layout doesn't uniquely determine it.
- **6 balanced armature drivers.** Role: not banded by AFUL, just called "six high-performance balanced armature drivers." `[VERIFY]` brand / model of BAs (likely Sonion or Knowles; not stated).
- **1 passive radiator (PR).** This is the unusual element. It is mechanically adjustable between **closed mode** and **open mode**, which gives the user two bass tunings. Closed mode: "solid, atmospheric, punchy bass." Open mode: "deeper, more elastic, and softer bass character."
- **1 micro planar magnetic driver.** Role: high-frequency treble extension, per the vendor description ("EST-like treble with wide extensions up to 35 kHz").

AFUL's marketing position is that the PR driver is the standout: it gives a "dual-mode bass" switch without using electronic switches or swappable nozzles.

## Manufacturer technology claims

These are claims as written by AFUL. They are vendor marketing language, not third-party verification. The skill should not invent corroboration, only note what AFUL says and flag it.

- **Quad-Brid Driver Setup.** "Step above the previous IEMs in the Performer series. Instead of a traditional DD + BA arrangement, we have a quad-brid setup here on the P8S." Translation: 1 DD + 6 BA + 1 PR + 1 micro planar = 4 driver types. The "quad-brid" framing is marketing.

- **Dual-Mode Bass Tuning via Adjustable Passive Radiator.** "The pair has an adjustable passive radiator system where users can use the PR driver in open or closed settings." Closed = punchy; open = elastic and softer. Vendor position: this is a unique tuning solution without switches or swappable nozzles. Whether the mechanical adjustment is repeatable, durable, or sealed against body-oil ingress is not addressed. `[VERIFY]`.

- **Patented RLC Network Crossover Technology.** "AFUL's signature RLC (Resistor, Capacitor, Inductor) crossover technology is implemented to get the best out of the 9-driver setup here on the P8S. The crossover is carefully adjusted to refine the 9-drivers perfectly, eliminating resonance peaks and ensuring a smooth, detailed, EST-like treble with wide extensions up to 35 kHz." Translation: the crossover uses RLC tuning rather than simple RC or LC filters. AFUL markets this as a signature tech. The "EST-like treble" descriptor is meaningful, since the micro planar is meant to give EST-like quality. `[VERIFY]` whether the RLC topology is genuine, novel, or a relabeled standard approach.

- **Advanced RESInator Micro-Resonance Technology.** "This tech uses ultra-precise 3D-printed micro-resonant channels that correct the non-ideal driver behavior and matches their performance across the frequency band ensuring a highly-coherent sound profile." Translation: 3D-printed acoustic pathways inside the shell that act like Helmholtz resonators to flatten driver peaks and match driver behaviour. AFUL markets this in conjunction with the RLC crossover as addressing driver imperfections. Likely real engineering (crossover networks + acoustic chambers are standard); whether it warrants the "RESInator" branding is a marketing call.

- **High-Damping Pressure-Balanced Technology.** Mentioned in the feature list with no body-text explanation. Likely refers to a multi-vent pressure-equalisation system. The Hifigo Q&A confirms there are two vents: a tuning vent for low-frequency tuning, plus a separate top pressure-relief vent with internal damping. Blocking the tuning vent does not increase ear pressure because they are independent acoustic paths.

- **3D-Printed Acoustic Pathways.** The shell uses high-precision 3D printing for both the medical-grade resin body and the internal acoustic structure. This is standard for boutique IEMs at this tier, not unique to AFUL.

- **"Classy Design Inspired by the Echoes of Jupiter."** Faceplates show swirling storms and cloud bands at Jupiter's North Pole, per the description. Aesthetic, not technical.

- **"Easy to Drive."** Per the vendor. With 26Ω and 108 dB/mW (or whatever the 108 dB unit is), it should be friendly to phone dongles. Worth verifying in actual listening, since "easy to drive" vendor claims vary widely in real-world behaviour.

- **"True HiFi Acoustic Response, Accurate Full-Range Sound Reproduction."** Generic marketing. Vendor-side.

- **"Fuller, Grande, Slightly Warm Sound Profile."** This is the most specific tuning claim AFUL makes. Worth noting it down for the write-up as the vendor's positioning.
